`arp` shows the Address Resolution Protocol cache — the local mapping your machine maintains between IP addresses and the physical hardware (MAC) addresses of devices on the same local network. Every device on a local network needs this translation to actually deliver data at the hardware level, beneath IP addressing.

`arp -a` is the practical, everyday use: a quick list of every device your machine has recently talked to on the local network, by IP and hardware address. It's strictly local-network-scoped — it has nothing to say about anything beyond your immediate network segment, which is exactly the boundary DNS and routing exist to handle instead.
