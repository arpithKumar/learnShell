`ncdu` (NCurses Disk Usage) scans a directory tree once and then presents the results as an interactive, navigable list sorted by size — letting you drill into the largest subdirectories one level at a time to track down exactly what's consuming disk space. It solves the same problem as `du -sh */ | sort -h`, but without needing to re-run the scan or manually re-sort every time you go deeper into a folder.

It's a particularly fast way to answer "what's actually filling up this disk" on a server or laptop running low on space, since the interactive browsing makes it easy to keep narrowing down to the real offender without repeated commands.
