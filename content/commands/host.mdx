`host` performs a DNS lookup and prints the result in a short, plain-English line — "example.com has address 93.184.216.34" — rather than `dig`'s more detailed, technical report. It's the quicker option when you just want a straightforward answer.

The two tools overlap heavily in purpose; `host` favors readability for simple lookups, while `dig` offers more control and detail for actual DNS troubleshooting. Knowing both means picking whichever fits the moment.
