`rev` reverses the characters within each line of its input — "hello" becomes "olleh." It's a small, specialized tool that doesn't come up often, but solves one particular problem neatly: extracting something measured from the *end* of a line, rather than the beginning, by reversing, applying a front-anchored tool like `cut`, then reversing back.

It's easy to confuse with `tac`, which reverses the *order of lines* rather than the characters within them — the two solve genuinely different problems despite the similar-sounding names.
