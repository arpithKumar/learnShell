`traceroute` reveals every network hop — every router — a packet passes through on its way from your machine to a destination, along with the time each hop took. Where `ping` answers a binary "is this host reachable," `traceroute` answers a more diagnostic question: "and if there's a problem, roughly where along the path is it?"

A hop that suddenly shows much higher latency than its neighbors, or starts timing out entirely, points to where a slowdown or outage is actually happening — useful information `ping` alone can't give you, since it only sees the final destination, not the path leading there.
