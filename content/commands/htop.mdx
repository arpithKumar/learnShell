`htop` is an interactive process viewer, functionally similar to `top` but with a friendlier, colorized interface: scrollable process lists, mouse support, and the ability to sort columns or kill a process directly from a function-key menu rather than typing a PID manually.

It's a popular upgrade over `top` for everyday interactive use, though `top` remains more universally available out of the box — on a minimal or unfamiliar server, `top` may be the only one of the two actually installed.
