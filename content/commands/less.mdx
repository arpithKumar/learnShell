`less` displays a file's contents one screen at a time, letting you scroll up and down and search through it interactively — a far better way to read a long file than `cat`, which just dumps everything at once and scrolls past.

Once inside `less`, the controls are simple: arrow keys or space to scroll, `/` followed by a search term to find something, `n` to jump to the next match, and `q` to quit. Because `less` loads content incrementally rather than reading the whole file upfront, it opens instantly even on files too large to comfortably `cat`.

A good mental rule: reach for `cat` when you want to *use* a file's content (pipe it, combine it), and `less` when you actually want to *read* it.
