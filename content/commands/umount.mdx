`umount` detaches a mounted filesystem from its directory, the reverse of `mount`. It's the proper way to disconnect a removable drive or network share before physically unplugging it or considering it safe to remove.

The reason this matters more than it might seem: unmounting flushes any data still pending in memory out to the actual device, ensuring writes are complete. Disconnecting a drive without unmounting it first skips that flush and is a common, avoidable cause of corrupted files on removable media.
