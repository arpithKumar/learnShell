`nl` adds line numbers to a file's output, similar to `cat -n` but with more control over exactly how numbering works — which lines get numbered, what separator appears between the number and the text, and how the numbers are formatted.

For the common case of "just number every line," `cat -n` is simpler to type. `nl` earns its place when you need something `cat -n` can't do, like skipping blank lines (its actual default behavior) or customizing the separator with `-s`.
