`iostat` focuses specifically on disk input/output activity, reporting how busy each storage device is and how much data is being read and written. It's the right tool when general monitoring (`top`, `vmstat`) suggests the bottleneck is disk-related rather than CPU or memory, and you need device-level detail to confirm it.

Run with an interval and count, like `iostat 2 5`, it gives repeated snapshots over time, making it easier to see whether disk activity is a sustained problem or a brief spike.
